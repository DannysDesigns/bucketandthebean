Calibration constants

βs: pull two shots one click apart with the same dose; βs = |ln(time A ÷ time B)|. βd: pull two shots at the same click with different doses (e.g. 17.5 g and 18.5 g); βd = ln(time 2 ÷ time 1) ÷ ln(dose 2 ÷ dose 1). The helper below does the math. Saved on this device and with every log.
